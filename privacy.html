Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the World News in 60 website and app handle.

This website

This website does not use accounts, forms, cookies, advertising or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

The World News in 60 app

Our app connects only to social media accounts owned by World News in 60, authorized by our account owner. Through those connections it:

  • reads basic profile information of our own account (for example its ID and username) to confirm it is posting to the right account;
  • uploads and publishes videos that we created ourselves, with their captions.

The app does not access, collect or store information about other users, viewers, followers or their content. It does not sell or share data with anyone.

How access is stored

The authorization tokens for our own accounts are stored privately and are used only to publish our videos. Access can be withdrawn at any time by removing the app in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions).

Children

Our app is not directed to children and does not collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: message @worldnewssixty on Instagram or TikTok.
